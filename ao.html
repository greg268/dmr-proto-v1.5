AO World: Record free cash flow drives return to net cash and £20m shareholder returns

Analysis by Roland Head  ·  Senior Financial Writer

An experienced financial journalist and investor with over a decade covering UK small and mid-cap stocks. I specialise in income and value investing, with a focus on companies generating consistent free cash flow. Learn more

17 Jun 2026 · 11:00am
15 comments 1,204 reads 51
Analyst's view: Cautiously positive↑ Upgraded from Neutral, held since Feb 2026

AO World has delivered a standout set of full-year results. Revenue growth of 11.4% in a competitive UK online appliances market shows clear market share gains, while adjusted pre-tax profit up 16.1% demonstrates operating leverage. The headline that most impresses me is free cash flow of £66.4m — up 152% year-on-year — which has transformed the balance sheet from £43.6m net debt to £16.4m net cash in a single year. The special dividend of 2.4p and £5m buyback confirm board confidence in the trajectory. I'm moving to CAUTIOUSLY POSITIVE: the shares have re-rated but further upside remains if this performance continues.

Background

AO World (AO.) is the UK's leading online electrical retailer, selling major domestic appliances, televisions, laptops and related products directly to consumers. Founded in Bolton in 2000 as a one-product price-comparison site, AO has grown into a fully integrated online retailer with its own logistics fleet, delivery teams and recycling operation.

AO's competitive advantage lies in its end-to-end logistics capability. While rivals often use third-party couriers, AO delivers and installs appliances with its own teams and removes old appliances for recycling. This creates materially better customer satisfaction scores (NPS of 83) and higher repeat purchase rates than competitors can achieve.

FY26 results overview

Results for the year ended 31 March 2026. The key headline figures:

  • Revenue £1,267m, up 11.4% year-on-year
  • Adjusted profit before tax £50.5m, up 16.1%
  • Free cash flow £66.4m (FY25: £26.3m), up 152%
  • Net cash £16.4m at year end (FY25: net debt £43.6m)
  • Special dividend of 2.4p per share declared
  • £5m share buyback programme announced
  • Customer Net Promoter Score 83 — among the highest in UK retail

Management commented: "We have delivered another year of strong revenue growth, significant profit improvement, and a remarkable transformation in cash generation." The company is now guiding to continued revenue growth and margin improvement in FY27, though specific guidance figures were not provided.

Financial review

The profit improvement was driven by a combination of revenue growth and operational leverage. AO's UK adjusted EBITDA margin improved to approximately 4.5% from 4.1% — modest in absolute terms but significant given the intensity of competition in online retail. Management targets a medium-term EBITDA margin of 5–6%.

The cash conversion story is the standout feature of these results. Working capital improvement contributed approximately £20m to the FCF increase, reflecting better inventory and payables management as the business scaled. Capital expenditure was £28m, primarily in logistics infrastructure — broadly flat year-on-year — confirming the FCF improvement is largely structural rather than driven by capex cuts.

The balance sheet transformation is significant: from £43.6m net debt to £16.4m net cash in a single year is a meaningful de-risking of the investment case. It also opens the door to a more regular capital return programme, with today's special dividend and buyback representing the first steps. Adjusted EPS of approximately 5.8p implies a forward PE of around 26x at current prices — not cheap, but AO has historically traded at a premium to UK retail peers given its superior growth profile.
